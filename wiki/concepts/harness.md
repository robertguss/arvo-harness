# Harness

The machinery either product builds: **agent loop**, **tool dispatch**, **TUI**,
**plugin host**, **provider adapters**. Shared domain concept across
[[entities/arvo|Arvo]] and [[entities/ore|Ore]].

Avoid calling it: CLI wrapper, framework (`CONTEXT.md`).

After v0.1, user-facing surfaces should name **Arvo** or **Ore**, not “the
harness,” for the product itself.
